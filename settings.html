Silly Teyvat

Off unloads everything Silly Teyvat adds, and SillyTavern works as if it were not installed. Your settings, cards and journeys are kept for when you turn it back on.

Features

Each switch turns a whole set on or off. A partly lit switch means only some of that set is on; the single settings are in the tabs below. A lit Paimon icon means the Silly Teyvat switch in the Paimon menu turns that set off and back on.

Residents

Every arrival meets at least one resident and one enemy, if the location has them. Favorites always appear, and so do characters you left behind unless more than 3 are there. Anyone else rolls this chance, up to 3 met at once.

Travel

The twins

Play either twin and you are the Traveler, while the other twin leads the Abyss Order and is found in the Chasm. Their Abyss cards are in the Prompts tab.

Trust

Each character's trust runs from 0 to 5 hearts in each chat. Characters earn or lose it through the roleplay and through gifts from your Inventory. Open Trust from the Paimon menu to see it, and to ask what they think of you. Only a new chat starts it again, and characters who already know the one you play start higher: set this per character in Character Customization → Starting trust.

Wilderness

One enemy from the waypoint's pool always appears. More can roll in, up to this many and never more than 3 met at once. Enemies you defeat stay away from that waypoint until you restore them from its map card.

Turn-based battles

Turn on Attack and send a message to fight a round. Dice fuel Elemental Skills and Bursts; Normal Attacks are free but can miss. Edit any moveset in Character Customization → Moveset.

System prompts sent with every roleplay reply while Silly Teyvat is on. {{char}} and {{user}} are filled in. Clear a prompt to turn it off; Reset restores the bundled version.

With Chat Completion, these prompts and the World scale, Combat state and Trust notes are also entries in AI Config → Prompts. Drag them there to choose when the AI reads them; editing a prompt in either place changes both.

Other prompts

Everything else Silly Teyvat asks the model. Words in single braces, such as {character}, are filled in by the game. Reset restores the bundled version. Clearing a prompt the game needs does the same; an optional note is simply no longer sent.
