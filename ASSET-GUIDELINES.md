# Silly Teyvat asset guide

Use these checks for every future character placeholder and location background. Review the full image and a contact sheet before importing it.

## Characters

Prefer official standing or introduction art with a plain transparent background. Avoid Wish splash art with scenery, large elemental effects, combat backdrops or decorative frames. A character's normal clothing, weapon or small signature companion can remain. Check actual alpha transparency rather than assuming a PNG is transparent. Do not use images with white rectangles or checkerboards baked into the pixels.

Search the official profile gallery, official introduction art and original game portrait assets first. Preserve the original artwork and record its exact image URL and source page. If a verified clean full-body image is unavailable, use a transparent official bust and identify it as a temporary small portrait. Never label a bust as a full-body sprite. Keep completed expression packs intact.

Cards have one main greeting and four alternate greetings. Use natural fan-written scenes, preserve player agency, distinguish canon from fan interpretation, and cite research sources. A home-based main greeting should start at the character's suggested home. Do not silently move an installed character's current or default map assignment when adding a card.

## Backgrounds

Use a floor-level or human-height view from a street, path, room or pedestrian platform. Do not use aerial views or high overlooks looking down across a settlement. Choose a nearby ground-level interior or approach when a clean street view is unavailable, and describe that choice accurately.

Exclude playable characters, game HUD, UID, party lists, dialogue, subtitles, cutscene captions, viewpoint panels and development overlays. Ordinary permanent world NPCs are acceptable. Do not erase an artist's signature or copyright mark. Verify the depicted location, not just the filename or search caption. Reject unrelated scenery even if attractive.

Keep the original image dimensions; do not invent resolution. Record clean low-resolution fallbacks. AI-assisted scene cleanup must be clearly labelled as edited, with its original reference, saved prompt and tool recorded. It is not an untouched official screenshot. No inaccurate source attribution.

Preserve existing location IDs, waypoint coordinates, filenames, recognition labels, resident assignments and saved scene paths during an image refresh. Update provenance only for users still using the bundled asset. Respect custom replacements and deleted starter locations.
