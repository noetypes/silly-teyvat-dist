# Starter location catalog

40 locations across the eight mapped regions. Scenery reviewed on 26 October 2026. Character assignments are optional fan VN encounter suggestions. Artwork belongs to its creators and HoYoverse. See [asset guidelines](ASSET-GUIDELINES.md) for future additions.

Coordinates, location IDs and user assignments are unchanged. Indoor scenes use the corresponding surface-map position. Some backgrounds show ordinary world NPCs; playable characters, HUD, UID, subtitles and development overlays are excluded. Original copyright marks are retained. Svetloledovka uses a disclosed AI-cleaned ground-level screenshot. Wanmin uses the nearby Chihu Rock plaza.
